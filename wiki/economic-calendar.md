The Council entered the week pricing a "disinflationary slowdown": inflation cooling (CPI 3.4%/2.5%), labor frozen (NFP -23K), consumer cracking (retail -0.6%, UMich 51.0). The 10Y dipped on the intervention; the signal is darker than the dip.

**Act III — The Growth Print That Changed the Conversation (Friday).** Flash composite PMI at **56.0 — a 52-month high** — with services at 56.8 and employment rising at the fastest pace since January 2025. GDPNow is tracking Q3 near 4.0% against a 1.5% Q2. The "slowdown" half of the thesis now rests on housing starts, continuing claims, and Walmart's kitchen-table consumer — while the measured economy accelerates. The 10Y closed the week at a **52-week-high 4.738%**, and SPY lost 1.37% — the market is re-pricing a Fed that may not be done, into a growth tape that says it shouldn't be.

**The Bottom Line:** Disinflation intact, slowdown in doubt, Fed hawkish-leaning, long end policy-managed. That is a **harder tape for multiples** than anything since the spring — and it lands exactly on top of core PCE (Wed), NVDA (Wed), and Warsh's first Jackson Hole keynote (Fri).
