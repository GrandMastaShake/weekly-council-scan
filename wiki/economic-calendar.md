**Week totals:** SPY **+1.27%** ($761.69 → $771.35) | 10Y **+18.6bp** (4.998% → 5.184%; daily closes 4.963 / 4.968 / **5.114** / 5.162 / **5.184**) | 30Y 5.331% → 5.504% | DXY **+0.8%** (100.21 → 101.04) | VIX **+0.4%** (14.81 → 14.87; intraweek high 16.57) | WTI **-3.2%** ($95.47 → $92.44)

**Narrative:** The prevailing thesis on this page is "no landing with a hawkish Fed." This week's data *confirmed* it on every front that matters, and the bond market treated it as a verdict.

Activity did not slow; it accelerated. The S&P Global flash composite jumped to 58.4 from 56.0, its fastest pace in over five years, and manufacturing surged to 57.0 against 53.6 expected. The KC Fed survey hit its best level in over a year. New home sales beat by nearly 70K units, and core capital-goods orders rose 1.6%, triple the consensus. Claims stayed below 200K for a second week. The one soft print, Richmond at -2, was the exception.

The inflation detail was worse than the growth detail. PMI respondents reported input costs rising at the steepest rate in four years, and UMich five-year inflation expectations edged up to 3.4%. Governor Barr then said more hikes are likely needed. The 10-year jumped 14.6bp on Wednesday, its largest one-day move in about 18 months.

What changed this week was *who* pushed yields up. The $70B 5-year auction tailed 3.1bp, the second-largest tail on record, with indirect (largely foreign) participation falling to 54%. The 7-year on Thursday was soft again. That is a term-premium signal: buyers want more yield to hold duration, independent of what the Fed does next. The 10-year ended the week at 5.184%, its **first weekly close above the 5.00% crisis line**, and CME odds for an October 28 hike rose to roughly 73-76% from ~58%.

Equities barely reacted. SPY rose 1.27%, led by a Monday chip rally, and VIX finished at 14.87. A 5.18% risk-free rate against a calm VIX is the week's key tension. The Trump–Xi summit changed nothing except adding a January 10 deadline.

This is the confirmation last week's page was waiting for. **CPI MoM > 0.3% carried** (August +0.3%; next CPI Oct 14). **DXY < 100 stays resolved** (101.04, a second weekly close above 100). **Oil > $100 stays resolved** (WTI $92.44). **NFP < 150K is AT RISK:** the Oct 2 consensus (+100K) sits *below* the threshold. Net: **two flags raised (unchanged count), with the 10Y flag escalated from "crossed" to "confirmed"** and one more (NFP) set up to fire next Friday.
