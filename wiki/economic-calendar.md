**Narrative:** This week was **"the soft inflation, hard consumer"** week — the mirror image of what the hawks feared. Inflation cooperated completely: CPI cooled to 3.4% headline / 2.5% core (second straight cool print after June's massive cool miss), and PPI came in dead flat. The pipeline is clean. The disinflation story survived its biggest test of the month.

But Friday flipped the script from inflation to demand. **July retail sales fell 0.6% MoM** versus a +0.2% consensus — a clean break of the −0.1% regime trigger and the worst consumer print of the cycle. Two hours later, **UMich August prelim sentiment printed 51.0** versus 55.0 expected — a 7.6% miss. Stack that on top of last week's −23K NFP and the picture hardens: the labor market froze in July, and now the consumer is wobbling in August. Issue #46 (consumer data shock) is tracking it.

**Act I — The CPI Exhale (Wednesday).** CPI at 3.4%/2.5% was unambiguously cool. September hike odds, already cut from ~54% to ~44% by the NFP shock, slid further toward ~30-35%. Growth stocks caught their second dovish bid in two weeks. SMH rallied 2.1% on the day; the 10Y refused to break above 4.70%.

**Act II — The Quiet Thursday.** PPI flat at 0.0% MoM — no goods-price pass-through, no tariff echo in the pipeline. Claims ticked up to 209K (first consensus miss after four straight beats) but the 4-week MA at 199K stayed below 200K for a second consecutive week. The firing end of the labor market is still quiet. It is the hiring end that broke.

**Act III — The Friday Consumer Crack.** Retail sales −0.6% and UMich 51.0 landed the same morning. Consumer discretionary (XLY) finished the week −1.4%, the only sector besides staples-adjacent names to lag badly; AMZN logged five straight red closes. The Council's consumer desks called it a shock (issue #46); the earnings desks immediately repriced next week's retail gauntlet (HD/TGT/WMT/LOW/TJX) as the confirmation test.

SPY closed the week at **$776.34** (+0.4% W/W), holding near highs — the index is still trading the dovish-Fed story, not the weak-consumer story. The 10Y finished at **4.696%** (+4bps W/W), DXY at **99.67** (third straight week pinned under/near 100), VIX at **14.25** — a six-month closing low. The market is pricing calm into the two biggest events of August: FOMC minutes Wednesday and Chair Warsh's first Jackson Hole keynote Friday.

**🚨 Regime flags carried + raised:** DXY < 100 (**broken**, third week), NFP < 150K (**broken**, −23K), and NEW — **Retail Sales < −0.1% (−0.6%, broken)**. The **Council Review posture stays in force** heading into Monday's session.
