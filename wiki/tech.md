**Catalyst 3 — Black Friday (Nov 27):** A half session. Chips led early; Nvidia turned red mid-morning (MarketWatch) amid reports of halted shipments to four countries and Blackwell racks found in Shanghai, and finished +0.8%. XLK +2.8%. Reuters' Friday preview called the bounce a possible "dead turkey."

**The Bottom Line:** Last week the market's question was trust. This week it got one answer, the CEO, and the stocks gave back half the relief within two days because the rest is still open: the reported SEC inquiry, export enforcement, a credit-rating review, and Huang's testimony to Congress.
