## THE WEEKLY NARRATIVE — What's Actually Moving XLY

> *This section is rewritten weekly by the Saturday Research Crew. It captures the story beneath the headlines.*

### This Week's Story: Tesla Up, Rates Up

XLY rose **2.04% to $119.98** while SPY rose 2.59%. The fund trailed the index for the first time in five weeks, and it closed lower every day after Tuesday.

**Catalyst 1: Tesla.** Tesla rose 8.9% and supplied about 1.7 of the fund's 2.0 points. Monday's 5.5% jump came with reports that it will end Cybertruck output around mid-2027 to make room for the Cybercab, and later in the week Bloomberg reported a Tesla–SpaceX merger could be announced as soon as December. Neither is about car demand.

**Catalyst 2: inflation came back.** October CPI rose 0.4% and core 0.3%, each a tenth hot, and PPI's annual rate reached 6.5%. Fed officials moved from holding to hiking: futures fully price a quarter point on Dec 9. The 10Y closed at 5.15%, up 29 basis points on the week, and the dollar index hit a 52-week high.

**Catalyst 3: the rest of the fund went sideways.** Outside Tesla and Amazon, the top ten roughly netted to zero. Home Depot and Lowe's rose about 1.5% each ahead of their prints; McDonald's, TJX and Booking slipped. DoorDash fell 4.5% and Lululemon 4.6%.

**Catalyst 4: mortgages fell anyway.** Freddie Mac's 30-year fell to 7.11%, a fourth decline, but the survey closed before Thursday's yield jump; next week's number is the one that will show it.

**Catalyst 5: the small caps reported well.** National Vision grew comps 3.6% and more than doubled adjusted EPS. BJ's, which fell 11% last week, turns out to have posted 8.1% comps and raised its year.

**The Bottom Line:** The long is up $1.82 and the stop is $4.90 under the price. Retail sales come Tuesday and the housing pair and TJX report Tuesday and Wednesday, into a 5% 10Y. **No trigger: the fund moved 2.0%, no major retailer reported, and no confidence print landed.**
