### This Week's Story: A Round Trip After the Election

XLY rose **1.91% to $117.58** while SPY fell 2.70%. It was the fund's fourth straight week ahead of the index, and its most volatile.

**Catalyst 1: the election.** Democrats took Congress on Nov 3. Futures fell overnight, then the market rallied on Wednesday after the result was accepted. XLY rose 4.26%, its best day since May 2025, to its highest close since January. Booking, Lowe's, Home Depot and McDonald's each rose more than 6% that day.

**Catalyst 2: Tesla gave it back.** Tesla fell about 3% on Wednesday, with coverage pointing to closer scrutiny of Musk's companies under a Democratic Congress, and then 10.8% on Thursday, for a cause I could not source. At 18.8% of the fund, its -10.4% week cost about two points. Amazon's +7.9% made up most of it.

**Catalyst 3: the prints.** McDonald's U.S. comps fell 0.2%, less than feared, and EPS rose 11%. Marriott raised its RevPAR outlook. Booking's room nights grew 7% against a 3–5% guide. Shake Shack grew traffic for a fifth quarter. Revolve fell 17%.

**Catalyst 4: the data.** Payrolls rose 165,000, more than double the estimates, and unemployment fell to 4.1%. Michigan sentiment rose to 51.0, and inflation expectations eased. The 10Y still fell to 4.86%, and the 30-year mortgage to 7.18%.

**Catalyst 5: oil.** WTI fell from $104 to about $90 on Friday evening's read, on bets the election would push Washington toward an Iran deal. That is the biggest tailwind for the consumer on this page, and it is not settled.

**The Bottom Line:** The long fired Monday, and the fund ended the week sixty cents under it with the 200D intact. Home Depot, Lowe's and TJX report the week of Nov 16. **No trigger: the fund moved 1.9%, no major retailer missed, and confidence rose.**
