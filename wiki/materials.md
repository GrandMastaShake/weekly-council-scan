**Sector Bifurcation:** Materials is a **three-headed beast** — and each head is telling a different story:

1. **Chemicals & Gases** (LIN, APD, DOW, PPG, SHW, ECL, CTVA, IFF, DD) — the largest group, driven by industrial demand, agriculture, and specialty chemicals
2. **Mining & Metals** (FCX, NEM, NUE, CLF, ALB) — copper, gold, steel, lithium — the commodity cycle
3. **Construction Materials** (VMC, MLM) — aggregates for infrastructure and housing

**The FCX Earnings Story:** Freeport reported Q2 on Wednesday (Jul 23) and the stock ripped +8.8% on the week to $63.50 — a dramatic reversal from the -4.9% slide of the prior week. Copper volumes and Grasberg performance apparently cleared the bar that the China GDP miss had set too low. FCX had fallen from $72.10 to $58.38 (-19%) in four weeks; this week's snapback is either the start of a repair or a dead-cat bounce inside a DXY-above-101 regime. The stock is still 12% below its 52W high, but the earnings reaction was unmistakably bullish — the kind of volume-and-conviction move that technicians notice.

**The SHW Earnings Story:** Sherwin-Williams reported the same day and got the opposite treatment: -6.3% on the week to $310.54, now 17.5% below its $376.95 high. Paint demand, housing exposure, and oil-linked raw material costs appear to have disappointed. The stock broke its 50D MA decisively and is testing the $310 zone — a break below $300 would open a run at the 200D MA. The divergence with FCX on the same earnings day is telling: the market is rewarding commodity exposure and punishing housing/chemicals exposure.

**The Gold Story:** Gold recovered +1.4% this week to **$4,067.60/oz** — a paradoxical move given the hawkish Fed backdrop. The explanation: the Iran conflict escalated further (oil now at $89, Hormuz still constrained), and safe-haven bids occasionally trump carry-cost math. NEM at $94.72, +5.6% on the week, is back above its 50D MA — the pre-earnings runup into Thursday's report (Jul 24) was clearly optimistic. At ~12x P/E with gold above $4,000, Cecil's value case is alive; whether the earnings delivery matched the price action is a question for next week's wiki.

**The Lithium Plot Twist:** ALB at $116.91, -3.2% on the week, -33% from its June high of $170.42. The lithium price recovery to CNY 152,000/tonne (+128% y/y) is now fighting the DXY headwind and supply-restart noise. ALB reports Aug 7 — guidance on the supply-demand balance is the only question that matters.

**The Steel Story:** NUE at $241.15, +1.9% on the week, +52% YTD, continues to grind higher on tariff-shielded pricing. Reports Jul 28.

**The Chemicals Story:** The oil spike to $89 means feedstock costs are rising fast for commodity chemicals (DOW) and coatings (PPG, SHW).
