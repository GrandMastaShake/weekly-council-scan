**Act III — The Hawk (Fri).** Copper had set an **all-time COMEX settle at $6.7115 Tuesday** (fading all week on LME/SHFE inventory builds; FCX printed a $79.91 record close Tuesday and gave it back to $76.45). The 10Y settled at 4.72%, three bps under the stress line.

**The Supporting Cast.** The ag complex extended its new uptrend: **corn hit a three-year high** after the Pro Farmer tour pegged the yield at 173.2 vs. USDA's 180.7 — CTVA +2.6%, IFF +4.4% to within 1.5% of its 52W high. **ALB -4.1%** took its first red week since earnings on lithium supply restarts (CATL's Jianxiawo permit, Bald Hill) and soft China EV orders. **KRO +7.3% printed a new 52W-high close ($8.91)** as the TiO2 squeeze passed +100% YTD. MERC bled a fourth straight week to $0.386.

**The Bottom Line:** A -0.7% week that resets XLB's RSI from 67.9 to 56.9 while holding every support is consolidation, not damage — but the regime underneath rotated hard: from fiscal debasement (DXY sub-99, gold vertical) to monetary hawkishness (Warsh, ~60% September) with a geopolitical wild card (ceasefire) that cuts dovish on inflation and bearish on the war-premium names simultaneously. The three rails are quieter than they've been in a month (DXY inside the band, 10Y three bps under stress, WTI $7 under the margin line) — and the loudest thing in the room is now the Fed. Next gates: **China August PMIs (Aug 31)**, **ISM Mfg (Sep 1)**, **August NFP (Sep 4)**, **Canada retaliation effective Sept 8**, August CPI mid-month, **FOMC Sept 15–16** — a genuinely live meeting for the first time this regime.
