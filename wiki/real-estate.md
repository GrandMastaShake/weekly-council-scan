## THE WEEKLY NARRATIVE — What's Actually Moving XLRE (week ending 2026-11-13)

### This Week's Story: The 10-Year Crossed 5% and the Fund Didn't Flinch

XLRE rose **+0.84% to $44.37**, its sixth straight up week, while SPY rose 2.59%. It trailed the index for the first time in six weeks but kept its lead for the year.

**Rates turned.** October CPI rose 0.4% and core 0.3%, each a tenth over forecast, and PPI's annual rate reached 6.5%. Fed officials moved from holding to hiking; futures now fully price a quarter point on Dec 9. The 10-year rose 29 basis points to 5.15%, crossing 5% on Thursday, and the 30-year rose to 5.48%.

**The fund held anyway.** Its best close was Monday's $44.64 and its worst Thursday's $44.36, a range of 28 cents in closes. The data centers rebounded, Equinix +4.1% and Digital Realty +3.3%, and Simon closed at a two-year high on Wednesday.

**The rate-sensitive names felt it.** Realty Income, Public Storage and Vivmark slipped. Safehold, whose ground leases are long-duration assets, fell 6.9% to a two-year low.

**Mortgages lag.** Freddie Mac's 30-year fell to 7.11%, a fourth decline, but the survey closed before Thursday's move. Next Thursday's number will show whether the 10-year's jump reaches borrowers.

**The Bottom Line:** The fund's six-week rally was built on a falling 10-year, and the 10-year just went back over 5%. **Trigger: a NEW 10Y break above 5%** (5.15% Friday close against 4.86%; the first Friday over 5% since Oct 16), issue opened. No other trigger: XLRE +0.84% (threshold ±5%); no REIT reported; no data-center leasing announcement found; no CRE refinancing default found.
