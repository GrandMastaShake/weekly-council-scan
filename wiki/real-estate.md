## THE WEEKLY NARRATIVE — What's Actually Moving XLRE (week ending 2026-11-20)

### This Week's Story: Yields Fell and It Didn't Help

XLRE fell **4.30% to $42.46**, ending six up weeks, while SPY fell 8.56%. It was the fund's third-worst week on the two-year bars.

**The 10-year came back under 5%.** It closed 4.91%, down 24 basis points, with 14 of them on Friday. A week ago the cross above 5% was the trigger. This week's reversal came from a flight to bonds as stocks sold off, not from softer inflation or a softer Fed.

**The fund fell anyway.** It closed lower four days of five and lost its 20-day, 200-day and 50-day in turn. Marky's stop at $43.09 broke on Thursday's $42.88 close; the position came off about flat.

**The AI trade ran through the data centers.** Digital Realty lost 9.7% and Equinix 7.7%, most of it Friday, as tech fell 12.5%. No leasing number or company headline was found behind either move.

**Towers fell hard too.** Crown Castle lost 9.3% and SBA 8.5%, with no cause sourced.

**The quiet names did their job.** Realty Income, Welltower, Vivmark and Public Storage lost 1–2%.

**Mortgages rose.** Freddie Mac's 30-year went to 7.28% from 7.11%, the first rise in five weeks, reflecting last week's yields.

**Safehold made another low.** It fell 6.1% to a two-year low in a week when yields dropped, which points to credit or liquidity, not duration.

**The Bottom Line:** The sector beat the index by four points and still broke its trend. **No trigger:** XLRE -4.30% (threshold ±5%); no REIT reported; no data-center leasing announcement found; no CRE refinancing default found; the 10Y's move back under 5% is not a new break above 5% or below 4%.
