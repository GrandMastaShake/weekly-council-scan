**Sector Bifurcation:** XLRE is not your father's real estate fund. It is split into four distinct groups:

**The Logistics Giants (The E-Commerce Play):**
- PLD (Prologis) — the world's largest logistics REIT. Warehouses, distribution centers, last-mile facilities. E-commerce = demand for industrial space. PLD is the Amazon supply chain in REIT form.

**The Cell Towers (The Connectivity Play):**
- AMT, CCI, SBAC — cell tower REITs. 5G rollout = more towers, more tenants, more revenue. These are infrastructure plays with long-term leases. AMT is the global leader. CCI is the U.S. leader (fiber sale closing H1 2026 is THE catalyst). SBAC is the pure-play tower REIT.

**The Data Centers (The AI Play):**
- EQIX, DLR — data center REITs. AI = massive demand for data center space. Hyperscalers are leasing every square foot. EQIX is the interconnection leader. DLR is the hyperscale leader. These are the fastest-growing REITs — and the most correlated to the tech tape.

**The Traditional REITs (The Bond Proxies):**
- PSA (self-storage), O (triple-net retail), WELL (senior housing/healthcare), SPG (malls) — traditional REITs with stable cash flows but lower growth. O is the "Monthly Dividend Company." WELL is a healthcare REIT benefiting from aging demographics. SPG is the mall REIT that survived the retail apocalypse.

**The AMT Earnings Story:** American Tower reported Q2 on Wednesday (Jul 30) and delivered a resounding beat. Net income surged **133% to $888M**, total revenue rose 4.7% to $2.75B, and property revenue increased 6.3%. Data center revenue grew **13.4% YoY**. AFFO per share was $2.71 (+4.2%). Management raised full-year 2026 guidance for the **second time this year** — midpoint property revenue guidance up $110M. The stock rallied +4.0% to $173.36. This is what earnings leadership looks like in a rate-pressure tape: the market is rewarding companies that can grow cash flows even as the 10Y spikes. AMT's international leasing stabilization and CoreSite data center outperformance were the stars of the show.

**The DLR Earnings Paradox:** Digital Realty reported Q2 on Thursday (Jul 31) and delivered what should have been a celebration. The market is asking: can DLR fund this growth without dilutive equity issuance at 4.74% 10Y rates? RSI at 59.1 is still not oversold — the stock may need more time to consolidate before the next leg.

**The WELL Pullback:** Welltower at $234.44, **-7.0% this week**, finally took a breather after a parabolic +33% YTD run. The demographic wave (10,000 boomers turning 65 daily) has not changed, but no stock goes straight up forever. RSI at 49.9 is now neutral after being deeply overbought. At 105x P/E, WELL is priced for perfection; the Aug 5 report needs to deliver occupancy and rent growth to justify the multiple.
