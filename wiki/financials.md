## THE WEEKLY NARRATIVE — What's Actually Moving XLF

### This Week's Story: The Steady Rebound

XLF rose **4.75% to $57.16**, against SPY +5.6% and XLK +7.5%. It lagged the market but was the steadier half of it: up all four sessions, while tech gained 9% Monday and gave 4% back.

**Catalyst 1 — Relief (Mon Nov 23):** Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang and the S&P 500 had its best day since April 2025. XLF +2.0% on 58.9M; GS and MS +4.2% each, the biggest gains among the seventeen names tracked here that day.

**Catalyst 2 — Rotation (Tue–Wed):** Chip stocks fell back and money moved into non-AI sectors. The dealers gave back about 2.8% over the two days; payments and regionals kept rising. Wednesday morning's sell-off, with a hot core PCE (3.3%) and a credit watch on Nvidia, took XLF to $55.33 before JPMorgan led a turnaround (MarketWatch, headline); the fund closed near its high.

**Catalyst 3 — The financing machine keeps running (Nov 24–25):** SpaceX priced a $44B GPU financing led by Apollo and Goldman; JPMorgan forecast $450B of AI bond issuance in 2027. Anthropic, the record IPO of the week before, ended its first full week about 11% over issue.

**Catalyst 4 — Friday (Nov 27):** A half session, +1.2%. The WSJ reported a management shake-up at Citi over its $35B bookkeeping error, and Bloomberg reported a settlement of over $400 million by banks facing a bond price-fixing trial.

**The Bottom Line:** The fund got back over its 50D and to its 20D without a single down day, which is better tape than tech's. But the rate setup did not improve: the 2-year rose, the curve flattened to +25bp, and the market priced a December hike at about 97%. The dealers, which fell most last week, rose least this week. The rebound is in payments and in a fear premium leaving, not in the lending business.
