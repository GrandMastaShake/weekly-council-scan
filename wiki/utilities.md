### Week of November 23 – 27, 2026: A Quiet Recovery

XLU rose **2.4% to $43.73** in a four-day holiday week. The market was closed Thursday for Thanksgiving and closed early Friday. The S&P 500 rose 5.6%, recovering part of the prior week's 8.6% loss.

**Everything went up.** All eighteen large utilities on this page gained. Duke Energy closed at a one-year high three times, and WEC once. These are the steady, regulated companies whose profits are set by state regulators, and investors have kept buying them through both the sell-off and the rebound.

**Interest rates stayed put.** The 10-year Treasury yield ended at 4.94%, almost unchanged and still below 5% for a second week. Utilities often move opposite to bond yields, but for a month now the stock market's mood has mattered more than rates.

**The AI power names did not recover.** Vistra rose 6%, but Constellation Energy rose just 0.3% and touched a one-year closing low on Wednesday. Both sell power on the open market and have been priced on data-center demand. That story took a hit last week and has not come back.

**Still close to a turning point.** The fund ended 18 cents below its 200-day average, the line traders watch for a change in trend. It failed at that line last week.

**Deals and courts.** Virginia's hearing on NextEra's purchase of Dominion ran past its planned three days; regulators must rule by January 11. No new court ruling was found for Edison's Eaton Fire cases or for PG&E.

**The Bottom Line:** A calm week for a calm sector. The regulated utilities keep making highs; the AI-linked power producers keep lagging. The fund needs one more good close to change its trend.
