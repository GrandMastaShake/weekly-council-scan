## THE WEEKLY NARRATIVE — What's Actually Moving XLP (week ending 2026-10-23)

### This Week's Story: A Print Behind the Bid

XLP closed **$88.26, +0.73%**, its third straight up week, after setting a **new 52-week closing high of $89.99 on Thursday**. SPY fell 0.43%.

**PG did the heavy lifting.** Procter & Gamble's Thursday report was the quarter the defensive trade needed: organic sales +4%, most of it volume, every category and region growing, core EPS $2.00 against roughly $1.89 expected, and the full-year guide held. The stock jumped 5.6% that day to a 52-week closing high, and the fund rose 3.3% with it. Coca-Cola, Altria, Mondelez, Kenvue and Keurig Dr Pepper all set 52-week closing highs the same day.

**Philip Morris stumbled and recovered.** PM's Wednesday print beat on adjusted EPS, but a currency-only guidance cut and a low-end operating-income range opened the stock 6% lower. It closed down 1.2% that day and -2.8% for the week.

**Rates helped.** The 10Y fell 19bp on Thursday alone and finished the week at 4.99%, under 5% on a Friday for the first time since mid-September. That narrows the carry gap the value desk keeps pointing to, from -237bp to -226bp.

**Friday gave some back.** The fund fell 1.9% on 19.9M shares after closing on its high Thursday. KO, MO and MDLZ all finished the week lower despite Thursday's records.

**The Bottom Line:** The rotation finally got a fundamental reason, from the sector's largest branded name. The next test is Coca-Cola on Tuesday, then Altria, Mondelez and Hershey on Thursday, with the FOMC in between. No trigger this week.
