- **Guidance:** Next-quarter revenue guidance was strong. The bar was ~$90B+ and Microsoft cleared it.

**Marky's Binary Read (post-report):** MSFT delivered the single cleanest mega-cap print of the quarter. A beat with strong Azure guidance launched the stock +14.7% — the biggest one-day gain for MSFT in years. After GOOGL's -8.5% fade, MSFT restored faith in the AI infrastructure narrative. The key lesson: when the whisper is above consensus AND the company delivers guidance above the whisper, the stock explodes. MSFT is the new safe haven in tech.

The AI capex is the wild card — moat or money pit? The wide analyst range ($6.46–$8.55) predicted uncertainty, and Meta delivered the lower end. The ad read is now lagging. After GOOGL's miss on Search/Cloud, Meta's miss on ad revenue is a second warning. The "sell the news" regime is real.
