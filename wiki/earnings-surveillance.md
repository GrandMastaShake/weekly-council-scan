## THE BIG 3: JULY 29–30 EARNINGS GAUNTLET

> *The most consequential earnings reports for the entire market. MSFT, META, AAPL, and AMZN together represent ~20% of the S&P 500. When they move, the market moves. Consensus refreshed 2026-07-26 via Yahoo Finance. GOOGL and TSLA have already reported — see Notable Surprises for the damage.*

### Microsoft (MSFT) — Wednesday, July 29, After Close

**Consensus EPS:** $4.24 (range $4.07–$4.89) | **Consensus Revenue:** $87.7B (range $86.9B–$91.2B)

**What the Council is Watching:**
- **Azure growth:** Consensus 39–40% YoY. Above 40% = bull. Below 37% = bear.
- **AI ARR:** Is Microsoft's AI business still accelerating? The market wants acceleration, not deceleration.
- **Cloud margin:** Azure gross margins have been expanding. Any compression signals price wars with AWS and Google Cloud.
- **Guidance:** Next-quarter revenue guidance. The bar is ~$90B+. Anything below $88B would be a shock.

**Marky's Binary Read:** MSFT is the most important earnings report of the week. A beat with strong Azure guidance launches XLK toward new highs. A miss on Azure growth triggers a 5–8% drop and drags the entire tech complex down. After GOOGL's -8.5% post-earnings fade, the bar for "good enough" has risen.

### Meta Platforms (META) — Wednesday, July 29, After Close

**Consensus EPS:** $7.22 (range $6.46–$8.55) | **Consensus Revenue:** $60.3B (range $59.0B–$62.8B)

**What the Council is Watching:**
- **Ad revenue growth:** Consensus ~+18% YoY. The ad cycle is turning — is Meta leading or lagging?
- **AI capex:** The market expects $60B+ in 2026 AI capex. Accelerating or moderating?
- **Llama monetization:** Is the open-source AI strategy generating enterprise revenue?
- **Reels vs. TikTok:** Reels engagement and monetization. Is Meta winning the short-form war?

**Cecil's Fundamental Read:** Meta's ad business is a cash machine — $40B+ in annual free cash flow. The AI capex is the wild card — moat or money pit? If Meta reports $7.50+ EPS and guides $8.00+, the stock breaks out. If ad revenue decelerates, it drops hard. Note the wide analyst range ($6.46–$8.55) — the street itself is uncertain. After GOOGL's miss on Search/Cloud, Meta's ad read is even more consequential.

### Apple (AAPL) — Thursday, July 30, After Close

**Consensus EPS:** $1.89 (range $1.83–$1.99) | **Consensus Revenue:** $108.9B (range $107.5B–$112.2B)

**What the Council is Watching:**
- **iPhone revenue:** Is the Apple Intelligence upgrade cycle real?
- **Services revenue:** The margin engine. Any deceleration is a red flag.
- **China revenue:** ~18% of revenue. Geopolitics and Huawei competition are headwinds.
- **Guidance:** The market wants iPhone AI momentum confirmed.

**Ophelia's Macro Read:** Apple is the consumer bellwether. A beat with strong iPhone guidance = healthy consumer. A miss on China and iPhone = weakening consumer. The Fed is watching Apple as closely as the market is. With GOOGL already disappointing on the AI narrative, AAPL's Apple Intelligence numbers carry the entire "AI consumer cycle" thesis.

### Amazon (AMZN) — Thursday, July 30, After Close

**Consensus EPS:** $1.82 (range $1.56–$2.10) | **Consensus Revenue:** $195.9B (range $186.0B–$200.1B)

**What the Council is Watching:** AWS growth (the margin engine), retail margins, advertising revenue, and capex guidance. AMZN has the widest EPS range of the mega-caps — the street cannot agree on retail profitability. After GOOGL's Cloud miss, AMZN's AWS read will either confirm cloud deceleration or restore confidence.
