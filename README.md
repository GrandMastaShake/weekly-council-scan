### The Headline

Stocks had their worst week since April 2025. The S&P 500 fell **8.6%** and is now in a correction, more than 11% under its October high, and all twelve of our sector groups finished lower, led by computer chips (**-17.8%**) and technology (**-12.5%**). The VIX fear gauge nearly doubled, from 19.30 to **35.89**, its highest close since April 2025. The corporate bombshell: NVIDIA reported better sales than expected, raised its forecast, and still lost **22.9%** in a week; the probe is reported by the press and has not been confirmed by the SEC or the company.

## How It Works

Every **Monday at 9:03 AM** (America/Indianapolis), the Council convenes:

1. **Cecil** (Fundamentalist) scores stocks on P/E ratios, earnings quality, and safety
2. **Marky** (Technician) reads momentum, moving averages, and volatility
3. **Ophelia** (Macroeconomist) tracks sector rotation, market regime, and risk-adjusted returns
4. **Consensus** weights each agent by historical accuracy, converges on a Top 5 portfolio
