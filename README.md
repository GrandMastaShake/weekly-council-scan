## 🌡️ Market Brief — Week of 2026-09-26

> *Auto-updated by the Synthesis Agent every Saturday. Data as of Friday Sep 25 close.*

### The Headline

The 10-year Treasury yield closed a week above 5% for the first time since 2007, finishing at **5.184%** (up 0.19 points). This time the push came from bond buyers as much as from the Fed. A $70 billion sale of 5-year notes had the second-largest "tail" on record for that maturity, a sign of weak demand. Business surveys showed the fastest growth in more than five years, and Fed Governor Barr said more hikes are likely. Stocks rose anyway: the S&P 500 gained **+1.3%** and the Nasdaq 100 **+3.2%** as chipmakers rallied on the idea that AI "agents" need ordinary server processors too (AMD crossed a $1 trillion market value and Intel jumped 13%). But the average stock slipped (the equal-weight S&P 500 fell 0.56%), and utilities closed below $40 for the first time since 2024. Oil fell about 8% to **$92.44** after U.S. and Iranian officials met for three hours at the UN. The VIX barely moved, closing at **14.87** after a brief Thursday spike to 16.57. The corporate bombshell: Oracle declared force majeure on its roughly $18 billion New Mexico AI data center because it couldn't get enough electricity.

1. **Cecil** (Fundamentalist) scores stocks on P/E ratios, earnings quality, and safety
