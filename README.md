## 🌡️ Market Brief — Week of 2026-09-19

> *Auto-updated by the Synthesis Agent every Saturday. Data as of Friday Sep 18 close.*

### The Headline

The Federal Reserve raised interest rates for the first time since July 2023. The unanimous quarter-point hike, to **3.75–4.00%**, came with a clear signal of more: **16 of 18 officials** pencilled in another increase this year. On hike day the 10-year Treasury closed above 5.00% for the first time since 2007 (**5.006%**), finished the week at **4.998%**, and four sectors (industrials, materials, staples and real estate) closed below their 200-day trend lines. The counter-story is that the consumer kept spending, with August retail sales beating at **+1.2%**. Chip stocks also shook off a Monday AI-slowdown scare (the semiconductor index fell 5.9% in a day) and finished green on a memory and processor shortage, pulling the Nasdaq 100 up **+0.9%**. The VIX spiked to **18.94** on Fed day, then fell to **14.81 (-6.5% on the week)**: the options market is calling the hike "priced in" while the bond market sits at 5%. The corporate bombshell was **Generac, up 30% in a day** on a **$2.4 billion** contract to supply backup power to Amazon's AI data centers.
