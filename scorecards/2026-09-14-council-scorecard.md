## Grading Notes

**Why B and not B+.** Three of four picks are well-evidenced and cite repo levels by file and line. DE is not: it was promoted because it passed a gate, not because its thesis beat the alternatives, and the card should not hide that behind a book-level average. The Council also booked its fourth consecutive single-sector Cecil pair -- structurally hedged this time, but still unresolved as a pattern.

**What the Council got right this week.** It blocked 24.7% of its own engine's consensus book on the blocked sector's own published bias, refused an available trigger level ($42.23) that would have let the picks through, and left the freed weight in cash rather than concentrating the survivors. Three separate opportunities to talk itself into a trade, three refusals. That is the behaviour the DOW rule was written to produce.

**What to watch Friday.** If XLU rallies on a dovish frame, the blocked utilities sleeve will be the best-performing thing on the board and this card will look expensive. That outcome is pre-registered in `shadow-book.md` and in Ophelia's journal, written before the answer was known -- so it gets scored honestly either way.
