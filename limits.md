# Limits Ledger

> A limit you keep re-blessing is a position you are afraid to admit you hold.

> **Living Document (created 2026-08-10).** Resting below-market limit orders. Every Monday, each ACTIVE entry gets a dated one-line re-justification against the current wiki/regime -- keep, reprice, or cancel -- and its `blessings` counter increments. Any entry reaching 3 blessings without a fill is flagged AUTO-CANCEL CANDIDATE and named in the report. Owner reports fills by editing the ledger; fill prices are logged so patience can be audited against paying market.
