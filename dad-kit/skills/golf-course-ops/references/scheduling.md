# Schedules: volunteers and crew

## What you need before building one

Ask for whatever is missing, one question at a time:

- The dates, and the shifts on each date (start and end times)
- The roles, and how many people each role needs per shift
- The people, and when each one can and cannot work
- Any rules: maximum shift length, minimum rest, who must not be alone, who
  needs training first, who is the lead on each shift

Common roles at a course (use only the ones he names): starter, ranger or
marshal, cart barn, bag drop, pro shop, beverage cart, grounds crew, and for
events: registration, sponsor signs, course setup, contest holes (closest to
the pin, longest drive), scoring, food and drink, cleanup.

## Rules for building it

1. **Coverage first.** Fill every required slot before thinking about
   fairness.
2. **Then fairness.** Spread early starts, weekends and the hottest shifts
   evenly. Count shifts per person and show the counts.
3. **Nobody double-booked**, nobody scheduled outside their stated
   availability, nobody over the maximum shift length.
4. **Gaps are listed, never hidden.** If a slot cannot be filled, it shows as
   OPEN in the grid and in a "Still open" list at the top.
5. **Daylight and weather.** First tee times depend on sunrise; frost delays
   push morning shifts later. Do not put a shift start before first light
   for course jobs. Build in water and shade breaks on hot days.
6. Check every date against its day of the week with code.

## Output

1. **Still open:** unfilled slots, at the top.
2. **The grid:** days down the side, shifts across the top, names in the
   cells. One page if possible, large enough to post.
3. **Per-person list:** each person's shifts, so he can text each one their
   own schedule. Offer the text-message version, one per person.
4. **Shift counts:** how many shifts each person has.

As a spreadsheet, use the Schedule sheet of the Golf Course Organizer:
Date, Day, Shift, Start, End, Role, Name, Confirmed, Notes.

## Changes

When someone drops out: show who is available and not already on, with their
current shift count, so he can pick. Do not silently reshuffle the whole
schedule; change only what has to change and list what changed.
