# Tournaments, outings and events

## Start with the basics

Ask for whatever is missing: event name and purpose (fundraiser, member
event, league), date and rain date, number of players expected, format,
start type, budget or entry fee, who is in charge, who approves spending.

**Formats** (explain in a sentence if he is unsure):

- **Scramble:** everyone tees off, the team picks the best shot, everyone
  plays from there. Fast and friendly; the usual choice for charity events.
- **Best ball:** everyone plays their own ball; the team takes the best
  score on each hole.
- **Stroke play:** everyone counts every stroke. Slowest; for serious events.

**Start type:**

- **Shotgun start:** every group starts at the same time on a different
  hole. Everyone finishes together, which suits a meal afterward. On a busy
  day some holes get two groups, called A and B.
- **Tee times:** groups go off one after another from the first tee, usually
  8 to 10 minutes apart.

## Planning timeline

Adjust to the event; a league night needs a fraction of this.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 12 weeks out | Date, rain date, format, budget, entry fee. Book the course date. Decide who is in charge of what. |
| 8 weeks out | Sponsor asks go out. Registration opens. Order anything with a long lead time (shirts, banners, sponsor signs). |
| 6 weeks out | Prizes and contest holes decided. If offering a hole-in-one prize, arrange the prize insurance now; insurers have specific rules about yardage and witnesses. |
| 4 weeks out | Food and drink plan and count estimate. Volunteer call. Rules sheet drafted. |
| 2 weeks out | Reminder to registered players. Sponsor signs to the printer. Volunteer schedule built. |
| 1 week out | Final head count to the kitchen. Pairings and hole assignments. Print rules sheets, scorecards, cart signs. |
| Day before | Signs placed, carts labeled, registration table packed, cash box and prizes ready, contest-hole markers out. |
| Day of | Run of show (below). |
| Day after | Results posted, thank-yous to sponsors, volunteers and players, money reconciled, notes for next year. |

Turn it into dated tasks in the standard task columns when he gives the
event date. Check every date against its day of the week with code.

## Pairings and team balancing (scrambles)

With handicaps: sort players, split them into A, B, C and D groups by
handicap, and build each team from one of each, so the teams are about even.
Keep requested groups together and balance around them. Show each team's
combined handicap so the balance is visible. Without handicaps, ask him to
sort the players into rough skill groups.

For a shotgun, assign each team a starting hole (and A or B if doubled) and
make a cart sign for each.

## Run of show (day of)

A one-page timeline: arrival and setup, registration opens, range open,
announcements, start, turn (food and drink at the halfway point), finish,
scoring, meal, awards, cleanup. Each line has a time and a person
responsible. Include the rain plan and the lightning policy on the page.

## Sponsors and registration

Track sponsors in the Events sheet: Sponsor, Level, Amount, Paid, Logo
received, Sign ordered, Thank-you sent.

Registration list: Name, Team, Handicap, Paid, Contact, Shirt size if
relevant, Notes (dietary, cart partner request). Mark who has not paid one
week out.

## After

Results: team scores, contest winners, and thank-yous. Then "Notes for next
year": what ran late, what ran out, what people asked for. It goes in the
workbook, so next year's planning starts from it.
