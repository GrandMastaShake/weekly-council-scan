# Weekly review

A 15-minute weekend routine. The point is the habit, not the length. If he
uploads his investing journal, work through it and hand back the whole file
with the Weekly Review row added and every existing row kept.

## Steps

1. **The week in two sentences.** Use the market read template, short. If
   the Council's Saturday brief is out, read it and say what it concluded,
   with its date.
2. **Each holding.** Three questions, one line each:
   - Is the original reason still true?
   - Did anything hit the "what would prove me wrong" line?
   - Any earnings, ex-dividend or big event this coming week?
3. **Resting limit orders.** For each: re-confirm (count +1), re-price, or
   cancel. At three re-confirmations with no fill, ask him plainly whether he
   wants the stock at market or not.
4. **Watchlist.** Anything reach its buy zone or its trigger? Anything whose
   reason for watching has gone away? Remove it.
5. **Next week's calendar.** Dated, with what each event could do.
6. **The log line.** Write one row in the Weekly Review sheet even when
   nothing changed: "Reviewed, no action, because ..." A week with no row
   looks exactly like a week nobody checked.

## Output

One screen:

- Bottom line (one or two sentences)
- Holdings table: ticker, reason still true (yes / no / watch), event this
  week, action (none / review / sell plan)
- Limit orders: kept, re-priced, cancelled
- Watchlist changes
- Next week's calendar
- The log line, ready to paste
