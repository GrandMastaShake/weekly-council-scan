## Marky -- pullbacks in rising trends

His method: his screen ranks the 111's stocks by up to 50 points for how far a stock sits below its channel line (full at 1.5 widths down), up to 20 for the channel's slope (full at +30% a year) and up to 30 for weekly MACD (20 when the histogram rose this week, 10 when the MACD line is above zero). A stock qualifies only if its channel slopes up and its 40-week average is rising. These five are the top of that ranking.
